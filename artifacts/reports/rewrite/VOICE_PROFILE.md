# Voice

Plain, direct, specific research writing. The author's stated preference is purpose → method/use → rationale → result, applied flexibly rather than as a repeated template. Prefer “we used,” “we found,” and “this comparison” when useful. Explain technical tools as workflow choices. Preserve uncertainty and distinguish observed results from interpretation. Paragraphs have one clear job. Vary sentence length; cut repeated explanations rather than replacing them with synonyms.

Avoid: powerful, robust, comprehensive, leverage, seamless, novel without evidence; “not just X but Y”; claims of farmer decision support or resilience outcomes that were not tested; stock broad agricultural openings; numbered task recaps in the conclusion.

Basis: current user instructions and the writing kit's Sardor voice notes. No invented personal voice samples or anecdotes.
